The compatibility surface

A catalogue of every VMS facility OpenVMX has taken on, item by item, on two axes: how far it is built (from absent, through stub and partial, to implemented and oracle-verified) and whether it does the real thing or only reports success. This is the shape of the gap, not a wall of green.

This is a self-reported map. Status and authenticity are the project's own assessment, catalogued in docs/compat/ and regenerated from that source on each release — not an independent audit. Facade-risk marks a surface that returns plausible success without doing the work, or fakes shared state per process; these are bugs the project actively hunts (invariant INV-6), and they are shown here on purpose. Source of truth: the register on GitHub.
